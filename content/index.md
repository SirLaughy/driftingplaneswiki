title: The Pocket Almanac of The Drifting Planes
---
Welcome to our humble project. Our aim is simple, to document the world that what we have named The Drifting Planes; a great number of planes and dimensions that make up all that we know. From the Material Plane of which we sit; with sub-planes such as the Feywild and Shadowfell; and to the great cosmos beyond.

My name is Turas and my darling wife Tailea and I are on the journey to map and share all the knowledge of the planes with you.
